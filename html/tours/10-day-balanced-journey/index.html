Hoi An · Da Nang · Hue

Experience more of Vietnam, with more time to connect, explore, and unwind.

Discover a deeper side of Central Vietnam on a journey that brings together the cultural charm of Hoi An, the coastal beauty of Da Nang, and the imperial heritage of Hue. Building on the experiences of our 7-day Vietnam Escape, this carefully crafted journey invites you to explore ancient landmarks, uncover centuries-old traditions, savor regional flavors, and experience the elegance of Hue’s royal heritage through a memorable imperial-style dinner. With more time to explore and unwind, you will get to enjoy a richer journey filled with unforgettable discoveries, personal wellness, and meaningful connections.

Everything in the 7-Day – Plus More

Hue’s Imperial Legacy

Explore the Imperial Citadel and royal tombs, discovering the history, architecture, and traditions of Vietnam’s Nguyen dynasty.

The Beauty of the Perfume River

Enjoy the tranquil riverside scenery and timeless charm of Hue, taking in the peaceful landscapes that define this historic imperial city.

A Royal Imperial Dining Experience

Step into the elegance of Hue’s imperial past with a special royal-style dinner inspired by the refined culinary traditions of Vietnam’s former royal court. Savor an evening that brings history, artistry, and cultural heritage together in a memorable dining experience.

The Living Heritage of Hue

Experience Hue beyond its imperial landmarks through the enchanting sounds of traditional royal court music and the tranquil beauty of the Perfume River. Discover the artistic traditions and cultural legacy that make this former royal capital one of Vietnam’s most distinctive destinations.

More Time to Restore & Rejuvenate

Enjoy a gentler pace with more opportunities for personal wellness, relaxed shopping, and spontaneous discoveries, allowing you to unwind and savor each moment.

Welcoming & Farewell Celebrations

Begin and end your journey with memorable dining experiences that bring everyone together over wonderful food, shared laughter, and meaningful connections.

Why Choose 10 Days?

One memorable week. A beautiful balance of discovery, indulgence, and renewal.

Created for women who want to experience Central Vietnam beyond its highlights, this extended journey offers a richer cultural experience without the feeling of being rushed. With the signature experiences of our 7-day escape at its heart, the additional days invite you to explore Hue’s imperial heritage, savor its distinctive royal dining traditions, and enjoy a gentler rhythm with greater space for wellness, connection, and unexpected delights.

Rest. Renew. Rejuvenate.

Stay a little longer and discover more of Central Vietnam, with extra time to unwind, nurture your well-being, and create meaningful memories along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Blooming Passage for?

Blooming Passage is designed for women in their 40s, 50s, and beyond who want to experience Vietnam more intentionally—with meaningful culture, wellness, connection, and time to simply enjoy themselves.

How many women are in each group?

We keep our groups intentionally small — typically 6 to 10 women — so the experience feels personal, relaxed, and connected.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Not at all. You can come on your own and meet new people along the way. Our small-group format creates connection while still giving you plenty of personal time and space.

Where will we stay?

You’ll stay in carefully selected premium boutique hotels, with daily breakfast included. The exact accommodations for your journey, including any hotel stays beyond Da Nang on the 10-day journey, are confirmed in your final itinerary. Package pricing is based on double occupancy: we’ll make every effort to pair you with a guest who is comfortable sharing a room, or you can request a private room for an additional charge.

What is included in my journey?

Every journey includes premium boutique accommodations with daily breakfast, airport transfers and ground transportation, scheduled sightseeing and entrance fees, the excursions and activities listed in your itinerary, a bilingual tour guide, a dedicated host, personal concierge assistance, and the signature wellness experiences included in your package. International airfare, travel insurance, gratuities, and optional services are not included. See What’s Included for the full details.
