Before your passage.

Which journey is right for me?

Choose from 7, 10 or 14 days. Explore each itinerary to find the balance of culture, wellbeing and time to rest that suits you.

Where do the journeys take place?

Our journeys explore Central Vietnam, from the coast of Da Nang to the lantern-lit streets of Hoi An and the imperial heritage of Hue. See each itinerary for the places and experiences included.

How large are the groups?

Travel with a small group of 5–10 women, with space for shared experiences and time for yourself.

Are wellness experiences optional?

Your wellbeing is personal. Additional medical, dental, beauty and wellness enhancements are optional, so you can choose what feels right for you. Check your itinerary for included experiences.

How do I choose a departure?

Browse the upcoming journeys on our homepage, then contact us with your preferred dates and journey length. We can help you confirm availability and the next steps.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Of course. Start a conversation with Blooming Passage to discuss the itinerary, your preferences or any questions before choosing your journey.
