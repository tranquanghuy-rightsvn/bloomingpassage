WELLNESS & LONGEVITY

Make Your Journey Your Own

Your Blooming Passage journey can be as restorative as you want it to be. Alongside the wellness experiences included in your itinerary, you can choose additional medical, dental, beauty, and wellness enhancements that feel right for you.

Whether you’d like to add a health check-up, enjoy a little more time for self-care, or extend your stay for additional services, rest, or follow-up appointments, we’ll help you create a journey that feels personal and unrushed.

Optional Experiences May Include

  • Comprehensive health check-ups
  • Dental examinations and cleanings
  • Dermatology and skincare consultations
  • Non-invasive beauty treatments
  • Hair and nail services
  • Additional spa and wellness treatments

These experiences are here to complement your journey - not define it. You’ll have the freedom to choose what feels meaningful to you, while still leaving plenty of room to explore, relax, and enjoy Vietnam.

Disclaimer

Your comfort and well-being matter to us. Our personal concierge is happy to help arrange optional health, medical, dental, beauty, and wellness services beyond those already included in your journey package.

These additional services are provided and paid for directly to independent providers in Vietnam. We’re here to make the arrangements easier, while your chosen provider will answer your questions and guide you through their services.

Blooming Passage does not provide medical care or guarantee treatment outcomes and, to the extent permitted by law, is not responsible for services provided by independent providers. Our goal is to make your journey feel seamless and supportive, so you can choose the experiences that feel right for you.

SMALL GROUP DIFFERENCE

We keep our groups intentionally small - typically 5-10 women.

Small groups create something special. There’s room to get to know one another, share a meal without feeling like a crowd, linger a little longer when something catches your interest, and enjoy experiences that feel personal rather than packaged.

You’ll travel alongside women with different stories, perspectives, and life experiences - creating a relaxed atmosphere where it’s easy to connect, laugh, linger, and simply enjoy being together.

  • No giant tour buses.
  • No racing from one attraction to the next.
  • No feeling like just another face in the crowd.

Instead, you’ll experience:

  • Thoughtful pacing.
  • Personal attention.
  • Genuine connection.
  • Shared experiences.

You’ll have the companionship of a group, without feeling like you’re part of a crowd - and enough breathing room to enjoy moments of quiet time on your own.

Before your passage.

Is Blooming Passage a medical tourism trip?

No. Blooming Passage is first and foremost a cultural, wellness, and personal journey. Optional health and longevity experiences are available for women who want to be proactive about their well-being while in Vietnam.

What is the pace of the trip?

Intentional, not rushed. You’ll have thoughtfully planned experiences, balanced with time to rest, enjoy the beach, explore at your own pace, or simply do nothing at all.

How do I get from the airport to the hotel?

Your journey begins with a private airport pickup and personalized welcome. Transportation is provided for scheduled group activities throughout your stay.

How do I reserve my spot?

Choose your journey and departure date, complete the booking form, and submit the required deposit. Your spot is confirmed once payment and booking requirements have been received.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Not at all. You can come on your own and meet new people along the way. Our small-group format creates connection while still giving you plenty of personal time and space.

What if I have another question?

We’d love to hear from you. Visit our Contact page and send us a message. We’re happy to help you plan your passage.
