MORE THAN A VACATION

Culture, connection,
and time for yourself.

Blooming Passage is for women who want more than a vacation. It’s an invitation to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and experience Vietnam in a way that feels meaningful, personal, and unhurried.

Imagine waking up by the sea, wandering through historic streets, sharing a meal with new friends, discovering local artisans, savoring Vietnamese flavors, and making space for the things that often get pushed aside - your well-being, your curiosity, your confidence, and simply enjoying life.

Da Nang is your beachfront home base, with curated experiences taking you through the heritage, flavors, and cultural treasures of Hoi An and Hue.

  • Time to wander.
  • Time to savor.
  • Time to rest.
  • Time to connect.
  • And time to simply be.

FOUR SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES

The experiences that bring Central Vietnam to life.

Every Blooming Passage journey brings together four distinctive ways to experience Central Vietnam—from its living heritage and local craftsmanship to its vibrant food culture and restorative moments by the sea.

01

Heritage Encounters

Walk through centuries of Vietnamese history and culture. Discover Hoi An’s timeless streets, explore Hue’s imperial heritage, and encounter the traditions that continue to shape life in Central Vietnam.

Discover. Uncover. Remember.

02

Artisans & Orchard Life

Step beyond the usual tourist experience. Meet local artisans, explore gardens and countryside communities, and discover the creativity, craftsmanship, and everyday beauty of Vietnam.

Create. Cultivate. Connect.

03

A Taste of Central Vietnam

Come hungry. Leave inspired.

Taste your way through Central Vietnam—from local markets and street food to hands-on cooking experiences, tea traditions, and memorable meals shared along the way.

Learn. Taste. Share. Enjoy.

04

Sanctuary & Self-Care

Make room to slow down and care for yourself. Enjoy optional spa treatments, skincare, massage, and beauty experiences, along with unhurried moments to relax by the sea.

Because sometimes, one of the most meaningful parts of a journey is simply having the space to breathe.

Rest. Renew. Restore.

CHOOSE YOUR JOURNEY

Two thoughtfully curated ways to discover Central Vietnam.

Planned departures March – April 2027

  • 7-Day Vietnam Escape “Curated Essence”

    Da Nang · Hoi An|7 Days · 6 Nights

    Price: $2,495 per person

    Meals & Experiences included|Intimate groups of typically 6–10 women

    An inspiring introduction to Vietnam’s culture, coast, and charm.

    Experience the beauty of Central Vietnam, from Hoi An’s lantern-lit streets and timeless traditions to Da Nang’s inviting coastline and Ba Na Hills’ breathtaking mountain scenery. Savor regional flavors, discover local culture, and enjoy moments of relaxation and rejuvenation.

    Thoughtfully curated to balance exploration, connection, and indulgence, this journey leaves room to slow down, embrace new experiences, and savor each moment.

    • The Timeless Charm of Hoi An — Wander lantern-lit streets, browse charming boutiques, and enjoy a leisurely cyclo ride through the ancient town.
    • Living Traditions & Local Artistry — Experience a traditional tea ceremony, silk weaving, and the beauty of local artisan crafts.
    • A Taste of Central Vietnam — Savor local specialties, learn to prepare traditional Vietnamese dishes, and enjoy a delightful dessert food tour celebrating the region’s flavors.
    • Countryside, Coast & Golden Discoveries — Experience traditional basket boats and Ba Na Hills’ iconic Golden Bridge, surrounded by spectacular mountain views.
    • Time to Rejuvenate & Indulge — Unwind with a facial, full-body massage, soothing foot and head treatments, hair wash and blow-dry, and manicure and pedicure.
    Explore Vietnam Escape →

  • 10-Day Vietnam Journey “Deeper Discovery”

    Da Nang · Hoi An · Hue|10 Days · 9 Nights

    Price: $3,295 per person

    Meals & Experiences included|Intimate groups of typically 6–10 women

    Experience more of Vietnam, with more time to connect, explore, and unwind.

    Discover everything in our 7-Day Vietnam Escape, with the added richness of Hue’s imperial heritage, historic landmarks, and a memorable royal-inspired dining experience. With more time to explore, connect, and unwind, enjoy a gentler pace and a deeper appreciation of Vietnam’s culture, traditions, and timeless beauty.

    Thoughtfully extended to offer a deeper cultural experience, this journey brings together exploration, connection, and indulgence, with additional time to slow down and savor each moment.

    • Everything in the 7-Day Vietnam Escape — Enjoy all the cultural experiences, regional flavors, coastal discoveries, and restorative indulgences of our 7-day journey.
    • The Imperial Legacy of Hue — Explore the magnificent Imperial Citadel, the royal tombs of the Nguyen Dynasty, and the scenic beauty of the Perfume River.
    • An Evening of Royal Elegance — Savor a memorable royal-inspired dining experience celebrating the flavors and traditions of Vietnam’s former imperial court.
    • More Time to Discover & Connect — Enjoy additional opportunities to explore local culture, browse charming shops, and embrace spontaneous discoveries.
    • More Time to Restore & Rejuvenate — Slow down and indulge in additional moments of relaxation, wellness, and personal renewal.
