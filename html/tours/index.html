WHAT’S INCLUDED

Thoughtfully planned. Effortlessly experienced.

Each journey brings together comfortable accommodations, curated experiences, and thoughtful coordination, so you can focus on enjoying your time in Central Vietnam.

  • Accommodations & Meals Enjoy carefully selected upscale hotels and thoughtfully curated dining experiences, with daily breakfast and the additional meals specified in your chosen itinerary. Package pricing is based on double occupancy.
  • Room-Sharing Options Solo travelers may request a roommate match, subject to availability. If a suitable match cannot be arranged, a single-occupancy supplement may apply. Private rooms are available for an additional charge, subject to availability.
  • Airport Transfers & Transportation Enjoy convenient airport transfers and comfortable ground transportation for the activities outlined in your itinerary.
  • Sightseeing & Entrance Fees Explore Vietnam’s cultural treasures and iconic landmarks, with admission fees included for scheduled attractions.
  • Curated Excursions & Activities Experience Central Vietnam through thoughtfully planned excursions, cultural encounters, and memorable local experiences.
  • English-Speaking Guide Discover Vietnam’s culture and history with insights from a knowledgeable, English-speaking local guide during scheduled guided experiences.
  • Dedicated Host & Trip Coordination Enjoy a dedicated host and thoughtful coordination throughout your journey, so you can relax and enjoy each experience.
  • Personal Concierge Assistance We can help coordinate appointments with independent health, dental, beauty, and wellness providers. Services and payments are arranged directly with providers.
  • Signature Wellness Experiences Unwind with selected wellness treatments included in your chosen package, as detailed in your itinerary.

Your journey is designed to make exploring Central Vietnam feel seamless, enriching, and personal - so you can spend less time managing logistics and more time enjoying the experience.

What’s included varies by journey. Please refer to your selected package’s detailed itinerary for specific inclusions and exclusions. Optional services, treatments, and appointments not expressly included in your package may incur additional charges.

WHAT’S NOT INCLUDED

A few details to know before you go.

To help you prepare for a relaxed and worry-free journey, please note that unless specified in your selected package, the following are not included:

  • International airfare and airline fees, including baggage, seat selection, and flight changes.
  • Vietnam visa or e-visa fees and passport-related expenses. Guests are responsible for meeting entry requirements.
  • Travel insurance, including medical expenses, trip cancellation, and trip interruption coverage.
  • Optional experiences and additional services, including wellness, beauty, dental, or medical services not listed in your package.
  • Personal expenses, such as shopping, souvenirs, laundry, medications, and gratuities.
  • Meals, beverages, accommodations, and transportation not expressly included in your itinerary, including extra hotel nights, early check-in, late check-out, and airport transfers outside the planned itinerary.
  • Unexpected or additional expenses arising from personal itinerary changes or travel disruptions.

We’ll take care of the details included in your package so you can focus on enjoying Central Vietnam, making meaningful connections, and savoring every moment of your journey.

Before booking, we invite you to review your selected journey’s itinerary and inclusions. If you have any questions about what’s covered, we’re always happy to clarify the details so you can book with confidence.

Before your passage.

Is Blooming Passage a medical tourism trip?

No. Blooming Passage is first and foremost a cultural, wellness, and personal journey. Optional health and longevity experiences are available for women who want to be proactive about their well-being while in Vietnam.

What is the pace of the trip?

Intentional, not rushed. You’ll have thoughtfully planned experiences, balanced with time to rest, enjoy the beach, explore at your own pace, or simply do nothing at all.

How do I get from the airport to the hotel?

Your journey begins with a private airport pickup and personalized welcome. Transportation is provided for scheduled group activities throughout your stay.

How do I reserve my spot?

Choose your journey and departure date, complete the booking form, and submit the required deposit. Your spot is confirmed once payment and booking requirements have been received.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Not at all. You can come on your own and meet new people along the way. Our small-group format creates connection while still giving you plenty of personal time and space.

What if I have another question?

We’d love to hear from you. Visit our Contact page and send us a message. We’re happy to help you plan your passage.
