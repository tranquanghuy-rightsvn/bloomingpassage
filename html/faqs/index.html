Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers to help you feel at home with Blooming Passage. Explore the essentials, then talk to us about the journey that feels right for you.

01 / About your journey

Who is Blooming Passage for?

Blooming Passage is designed for women in their 40s, 50s, and beyond who want to experience Vietnam more intentionally—with meaningful culture, wellness, connection, and time to simply enjoy themselves.

How many women are in each group?

We keep our groups intentionally small - typically 5 to 10 women - so the experience feels personal, relaxed, and connected.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Not at all. You can come on your own and meet new people along the way. Our small-group format creates connection while still giving you plenty of personal time and space.

Is Blooming Passage a medical tourism trip?

No. Blooming Passage is first and foremost a cultural, wellness, and personal journey. Optional health and longevity experiences are available for women who want to be proactive about their well-being while in Vietnam.

What is the pace of the trip?

Intentional, not rushed. You’ll have thoughtfully planned experiences, balanced with time to rest, enjoy the beach, explore at your own pace, or simply do nothing at all.

02 / Planning your passage

Where will we stay?

You’ll stay in carefully selected five-star hotels, with daily breakfast included. The exact accommodations for your journey, including any hotel stays beyond Da Nang on the 10-day and 14-day journeys, are confirmed in your final itinerary. Package pricing is based on double occupancy: we’ll make every effort to pair you with a guest who is comfortable sharing a room, or you can request a private room for an additional charge.

What is included in my journey?

Every journey includes five-star accommodations with daily breakfast, airport transfers and ground transportation, scheduled sightseeing and entrance fees, the excursions and activities listed in your itinerary, a bilingual tour guide, a dedicated host, personal concierge assistance, and the signature wellness experiences included in your package. International airfare, travel insurance, gratuities, and optional services are not included. See What’s Included for the full details.

What wellness, self-care, and health experiences are available?

Depending on your journey, you may choose from massage, facials, beauty and personal-care services, skin consultations, dental care, and optional preventive health experiences. Your well-being is part of the journey - but these services are always your choice.

How do I get from the airport to the hotel?

Your journey begins with a private airport pickup and personalized welcome. Transportation is provided for scheduled group activities throughout your stay.

How do I reserve my spot?

Our 2027 departure dates are tentative while we finalize the details with our local partners, so we aren’t taking bookings or deposits just yet. Choose the date and journey you’re interested in and select Plan Your Journey, or contact us. We’ll be in touch, and we’ll let you know as soon as bookings open.

What if I have another question?

We’d love to hear from you. Visit our Contact page and send us a message. We’re happy to help you plan your passage.

Still wondering? Let’s talk.

Share your questions, your preferred dates and what you would love from your time in Vietnam. We will help you explore your options.
