Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers to help you feel at home with Blooming Passage. Explore the essentials, then talk to us about the journey that feels right for you.

01 / About your journey

What’s included in my journey?

Your journey includes boutique accommodations with breakfast, airport transfers, ground transportation, guided sightseeing, entrance fees, a bilingual guide, a dedicated host, personal concierge assistance, and the experiences listed in your itinerary. International airfare, travel insurance, gratuities, and optional services are not included. See What’s Included for details.

Where will we stay?

You’ll stay in carefully selected boutique hotels, with daily breakfast included. The 10-day journey also includes overnight stays outside Da Nang. Pricing is based on double occupancy, and we’ll do our best to pair solo travelers with a compatible roommate. Private rooms may be available for an additional charge.

What is the pace of the trip?

Our journeys balance cultural exploration, local experiences, and time to relax. Some days are busier than others, with walking and excursions involved. Your itinerary outlines each day’s activities so you can choose the journey that best suits you.

What wellness and cultural experiences can I enjoy?

Enjoy a thoughtful mix of self-care and cultural discovery, from massages and facials to local cuisine, cooking classes, and excursions. Optional skin consultations, dental care, and preventive health consultations may also be arranged. Your itinerary outlines which experiences are included and which are optional.

Is Blooming Passage a medical tourism trip?

No. Blooming Passage is a boutique travel experience centered on cultural discovery, relaxation, and personal well-being. Optional health, dental, or skin consultations can be coordinated upon request, but medical providers handle consultations, recommendations, treatment decisions, and payments directly with guests.

Can you accommodate allergies or special requirements?

Please let us know about any food allergies, dietary needs, mobility concerns, or other requirements when you inquire. We’ll do our best to accommodate them, but some requests depend on local availability and cannot be guaranteed.

How many women are in each group?

We keep our groups small and personal, with a maximum of 10 women per departure. This allows for a more relaxed experience, meaningful connections, and the flexibility to enjoy Vietnam together.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Not at all! Many women travel on their own, and you’re warmly welcome to join us solo. Our small-group journeys offer opportunities to connect with like-minded women while still enjoying time for yourself. If you prefer to share a room, we’ll do our best to pair you with a compatible roommate.

Can I have my own room?

Yes, private rooms may be available for an additional charge, subject to availability. Please let us know your preference when you inquire so we can discuss the options.

What if I have another question about the journey?

We’re happy to help. Visit our Contact Us page and send us a message. We’ll do our best to answer your questions and help you prepare for your journey.

02 / Planning your passage

How do I reserve my spot?

Explore our Signature Journeys and Travel Dates, then submit an inquiry for your preferred journey. We’ll guide you through the next steps. Your booking is confirmed once we accept your reservation and receive your required deposit.

What is the payment schedule?

Our payment plan has three installments: 30% to secure your booking, followed by two payments of 35% each. Please review our Payment & Booking Terms for payment deadlines and details.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Our Cancellation & Refund Policy explains cancellation deadlines, applicable fees, and refund eligibility. Please review them carefully before booking.

What happens if a departure doesn’t meet the minimum group size?

Each departure requires at least six paying guests. If the minimum isn’t met, we’ll contact you personally to explain the available options in accordance with our Terms & Conditions.

What happens if Blooming Passage cancels my departure?

We’ll notify you as soon as possible and explain the options available under our Terms & Conditions. We recommend waiting until your departure is confirmed before purchasing nonrefundable flights.

When should I book my flights?

We recommend waiting until your departure is confirmed before purchasing flights. Once your flights are booked, share your itinerary with us so we can coordinate your included airport transfer.

How do I get from the airport to the hotel?

Your journey includes airport pickup and drop-off in Da Nang. Once you’ve booked your flights, simply share your flight details with us so we can coordinate your arrival and transfer to the hotel.

Do U.S. travelers need a visa for Vietnam?

Yes. U.S. citizens generally need a visa to visit Vietnam. Check eligibility and apply through the official Vietnam e-Visa website. Please also review the latest U.S. Department of State travel guidance before departure.

Is travel insurance required?

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, emergency evacuation, trip cancellation or interruption, and travel delays. Review the policy carefully to ensure it meets your needs.

What if I have another question about planning my passage?

We’re happy to help. Visit our Contact Us page and send us a message. We’ll do our best to answer your questions and help you prepare for your journey.
