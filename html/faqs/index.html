FAQs

Before your passage.

A few answers to help you feel at home with Blooming Passage. Explore the essentials, then talk to us about the journey that feels right for you.

01 / Your journey

Which journey is right for me?

Choose the 7-Day Curated Essence for a gentle introduction, the 10-Day Balanced Journey for more time to explore and unwind, or the 14-Day Deep Pause for a longer, thoughtfully paced escape. Compare the itineraries to find your balance of discovery, wellbeing and rest.

Where do the journeys take place?

Our journeys explore Central Vietnam, with Da Nang as a coastal base and experiences in Hoi An and Hue. Each itinerary offers its own blend of local culture, cuisine, craft and time to slow down.

How large are the groups?

We travel in small groups of 5–10 women, creating space for meaningful connection and a more personal experience. You can enjoy shared moments while still taking time for yourself.

Can I join if I am travelling on my own?

If you are considering a journey on your own, get in touch. We can talk through the small-group experience, room options and the itinerary so you can decide what feels right for you.

02 / Planning your passage

What is included in my journey?

Inclusions vary by journey. Open your chosen tour to review the day-by-day itinerary, accommodation, meals and included experiences. Contact us to confirm the details of your departure before booking.

Are wellness experiences optional?

Your wellbeing is personal. Additional wellness, beauty and personal-care enhancements are optional, so you can choose what feels right for you. Check your selected itinerary for the experiences already included.

How do I choose a departure?

Browse the upcoming departures on our homepage, then open the journey you are interested in. Check availability on the tour page or contact us to confirm your preferred dates and group availability.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Of course. Send us a message through the Contact page with your preferred journey, dates and any questions. We can talk through the itinerary and practical details before you decide.

Still wondering? Let’s talk.

Share your questions, your preferred dates and what you would love from your time in Vietnam. We will help you explore your options.
