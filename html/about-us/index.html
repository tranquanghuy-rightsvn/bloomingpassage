About Us

Intentional travel. A journey back to yourself.

Reclaiming
Your Vitality.

When was the last time you felt completely heard, cared for, and informed by your doctor?

If you are like most women, navigating healthcare at home feels like an uphill battle of rushed 10-minute visits, complex patient portals, and unanswered questions.

For a long time, I spent years putting everyone and everything else first – my career, my family, my daily responsibilities – while placing my own well-being on the back burner.

Then, a sudden, unexpected health scare completely stopped me in my tracks.

Realizing I needed clarity and a deep mental, emotional, and physical reset instead of endless waiting rooms, I took a leap of faith and traveled to Vietnam on my own for the first time.

That journey changed everything.

In just days, I completed a comprehensive executive physical and sat down face-to-face with providers who walked me through my scans and bloodwork line by line. For the first time, I walked away with complete peace of mind, knowing exactly where I stood health-wise and how to naturally support my body moving forward.

After sharing my experience with my closest girlfriends, their encouragement gave me the courage to create Blooming Passage.

I created Blooming Passage for women who are ready to make space for themselves – to pause, reconnect, and experience something meaningful at their own pace.

If you’re in a season of life where you’re ready to put yourself back on the list, I hope you’ll join me in Central Vietnam.

Come curious. Come open. Come as you are.

Just come ready to explore, to slow down, to try something new, and to see what you might discover along the way.

THE FOUR PILLARS

Four guiding principles.
Woven into every journey.

These four guiding principles are woven into every Blooming Passage journey.

Intention

Make space for yourself.

Give yourself permission to step away from the demands of everyday life and pay attention to what you need.

Longevity

Become more informed about your health.

Explore proactive wellness and personal health awareness through thoughtfully selected experiences and optional health services.

Discovery

Go beyond sightseeing.

Experience the people, food, craftsmanship, landscapes, and traditions that make Central Vietnam so special.

Connection

Travel alongside women who understand this season of life.

Small groups. Shared experiences. Meaningful conversations. New friendships. Connection that continues long after the journey ends.

Small by design.
Meaningful by nature.

Six to ten women. A thoughtful pace. Space to be yourself.
Our journeys make room for shared meals, good conversations and the quiet moments in between.

Room for your own rhythm.

A small group makes it easier to feel at ease, share an experience and get to know the people beside you. It also leaves room for personal attention and a pace that feels comfortable.

Join a conversation, wander a little longer, or enjoy a quiet pause. Togetherness and personal space can be part of the same journey.

Care for yourself.
In your own way.

Wellness, beauty and optional proactive health experiences can sit alongside culture and discovery. Choose what feels right for you, with room for rest, curiosity and the simple pleasures of being away.

A little more room to rest.

Unhurried mornings, restorative experiences and time by the sea. Wellbeing can be as simple as making space in your day.

Personal. Optional.
Entirely your choice.

Your journey comes first. Wellness, beauty and proactive health experiences are optional, so you can choose how much to include and what to leave open.

Rooted in place.
Open to discovery.

Central Vietnam is at the heart of our passage. Da Nang offers the sea and space to breathe; Hoi An brings living heritage, craft and local flavors; Hue invites a quieter encounter with history. We explore each place with time to notice the people and everyday details along the way.
